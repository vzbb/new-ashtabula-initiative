## AidFlow Navigator — MVP Assessment Sprint (2026-07-04)

**Slug:** `aidflow`
**Site:** 211 Ashtabula Benefits Navigator
**Target Buyer:** United Way of Ashtabula County / 211 Ashtabula County
**Live URL:** https://new-ashtabula-initiative.vercel.app/aidflow/

### Current State

This is one of the strongest MVPs in the portfolio. Scores from the automated analysis: Design 9/10, Branding 9/10, Clarity 10/10, White-Label Fit 8/10. The site effectively extends the 211 helpline into a self-service digital experience. Branding leverages established United Way/211 trust signals. Content is highly specific to Ashtabula County — real programs (SNAP, WIC, utility assistance), real contact info (1-800-874-8545, 211@accaa.org), real partner agencies.

### Fresh Assessment Notes (Harriet's Own Review)

After reviewing the live screenshot directly, I confirm the automated analysis is accurate but missed several enhancement opportunities:

1. **"Demo" language live on production** — The 24/7 SUPPORT section reads "This demo keeps the 211 call center..." — this is confusing UX on what the page title claims is a live navigator. Remove or rephrase for production.

2. **Input field styling feels dated** — The "WHO ARE YOU SEEKING HELP FOR?" / "HOW URGENT IS THE SITUATION?" / "PREFERRED 211 FOLLOW-UP" selector fields use basic form styling that looks like Bootstrap 4. These could be upgraded to a modern chip/button selector pattern.

3. **Raster logo quality** — The "2-1-1 Ashtabula County" logo appears slightly pixelated. A vector (SVG) version would be sharper and smaller.

4. **Triage categories lack visual cues** — The four triage categories (Food, Utilities, Health, Housing) are text-forward. Adding icons or a card-carousel interaction with hover states would make the experience more engaging for stressed users.

5. **No progress persistence** — Users in distress may not complete their journey in one session. A localStorage-based "Save My Progress" feature would show genuine empathy.

### Recommended Improvements (3 Specific, Actionable Items)

#### 1. 🎨 Replace Triage Text Categories with an Interactive Card Wizard
**Current:** Four plain text categories ("Food," "Utilities," "Health," "Housing") with basic checkbox interaction.
**Enhancement:** Convert to a visually engaging card carousel with custom icons, subtle hover animations, and a "wizard" progress indicator. Each card should use a distinct accent color (green for Food, orange for Utilities, blue for Health, purple for Housing). Add a "Continue" button that transitions smoothly between steps. This transforms the first touchpoint from a form into an experience.
**Effort:** Medium | **Impact:** High — first-impression transformation
**Assignee:** nai-dev (implementation), nai-creative (icon assets)

#### 2. 📊 Add an "Our Impact" Stats Bar with Real 211 Ashtabula County Data
**Current:** No social proof or impact metrics on the page.
**Enhancement:** Source real 211 metrics (call volume, residents served, average time-to-benefit, programs accessed) from United Way annual reports or 211 dashboards. Build a subtle animated stats bar below the hero: "📞 12,000+ Calls Answered Last Year | 🏠 3,400 Housing Referrals Made | ⚡ 72% Benefits Connected Within 48 Hours." This gives United Way a concrete ROI story when they evaluate the tool — it's already telling their impact story.
**Effort:** Small | **Impact:** High — builds trust and closes deals
**Assignee:** nai-creative (research/verify stats), nai-dev (implement stats bar)

#### 3. 💾 Implement "Save My Progress" with LocalStorage Resume
**Current:** No persistence — if a user closes the tab, they lose their triage selections and program exploration.
**Enhancement:** Add a subtle "Your progress is saved automatically" indicator. On return, show a gentle banner: "Welcome back — you were exploring Food Assistance. Continue where you left off?" This is a small feature with outsized empathy impact. For a population that may be using library computers or shared devices, the psychological safety of "you won't lose this" is meaningful.
**Effort:** Small-Medium | **Impact:** High — differentiates from every other benefits tool
**Assignee:** nai-dev

#### 4. 🔍 Fix "Demo" Language and Upgrade Selectors (Quick Win)
**Current:** "This demo keeps the 211 call center..." text and basic form selects.
**Enhancement:** Replace demo language with production-ready copy: "This tool works alongside the 211 call center..." and convert the three selector dropdowns to a modern pill/chip selector pattern with clear active states.
**Effort:** Small | **Impact:** Medium — production polish
**Assignee:** nai-dev

### Priority: MEDIUM

The site is already excellent and sellable as-is. These are enhancement-level improvements that elevate it from "professional MVP" to "premium product." None are blockers to a sales conversation — United Way/211 could be shown this today and it would impress.
