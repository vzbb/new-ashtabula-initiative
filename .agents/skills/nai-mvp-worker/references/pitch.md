# Pitch Gate

- Read only accepted research, brand, implementation, and verification evidence.
- Match buyer facts, product behavior, route, contact details, and limitations.
- Prepare only packet-listed pitch and contact artifacts.
- Distinguish working functionality from roadmap ideas.
- Do not imply the buyer commissioned, owns, endorsed, or approved the product.
- Passing means outreach-ready, not permission to send.
- Do not send email, submit forms, place calls, publish posts, or schedule
  meetings without separate authorization.

Return the pitch package and/or verified contact artifact as evidence for
the orchestrator's review.
