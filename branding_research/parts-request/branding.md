# Parts Request (Lakeshore Auto Parts) — Brand Identity & Visual Specification

**Project:** Parts Request — Online Parts Ordering Portal
**Organization:** Lakeshore Auto Parts LLC
**Target Audience:** Commercial garage owners, DIY mechanics, fleet maintenance managers, marine/agricultural parts buyers in Ashtabula County
**Date:** 2026-07-04
**Creative Lead:** Rhodes (NAI Creative & Research)

---

## Brand Narrative

Parts Request is the digital storefront for Lakeshore Auto Parts — Ashtabula's independent auto parts counter that's been keeping local vehicles on the road since before the chains moved in. While O'Reilly, AutoZone, Advance, and NAPA have glossy corporate websites, Lakeshore has something they can't buy: local knowledge, personal relationships, and a counter staff that knows your truck by name.

Parts Request bridges that gap. It gives Larry and his team a modern web presence so customers can request parts 24/7, check availability before driving in, and submit commercial orders without waiting on hold. It's not trying to be Amazon for auto parts — it's a better way to do business with the guys who already know what you need.

**Core message:** "The auto parts counter that knows your car, your town, and your name."

**Taglines:**
- "The parts you need, when you need them." *(hero tagline)*
- "Ashtabula's local auto parts counter." *(brand tagline, from logo)*
- "Fast turnaround. Quality guaranteed." *(trust statement)*
- "Commercial Accounts Welcome — Skip the line." *(commercial pitch)*

**Value proposition:** A 24/7 online parts request system that captures leads after hours, streamlines commercial ordering, and gives Lakeshore the digital presence it needs to compete with national chains — without the owner having to learn web development.

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice: "The counter guy who actually knows his stuff."

The Parts Request brand speaks like a veteran parts counterman — direct, knowledgeable, no-nonsense, with a hint of blue-collar pride. It never sounds like a corporate marketing department.

| Attribute | Guideline | Example |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Direct** | Say what you mean. No filler. | "Need a part? Tell us what you're driving." |
| **Confident** | Knows the product and the territory | "We stock Ashtabula's most common repairs — brakes, suspension, electrical. If we don't have it, we can get it by tomorrow." |
| **Local** | Ashtabula references, not generic | "Serving Ashtabula, Conneaut, Geneva, and Saybrook." |
| **No fluff** | Skip the marketing speak | Not "Leverage our synergistic auto parts ecosystem." Say "We've got the part. Or we'll get it." |
| **Helpful** | Anticipates what the customer needs | "Don't know the part number? Describe what you need and we'll find it." |

### Key Phrases to Use

- "Fast turnaround" — speed is a competitive advantage over shipping from RockAuto/Amazon
- "Quality guaranteed" — Auto Value network parts with warranty
- "Ask for Larry" — personal touch, the owner is accessible
- "Ashtabula's own" — local pride differentiator
- "The parts you need, when you need them" — reliability message
- "We deliver to your shop" — convenience for commercial accounts
- "Commercial accounts welcome" — B2B sales channel

### Words to Avoid

innovative · synergy · disruptive · next-gen · AI-powered · smart platform · ecosystem · leverage · optimize · solution (generic) · revolutionize

### Pain Point Copy Angles

| Customer Pain Point | Copy Response |
|--------------------|---------------|
| "Tired of being put on hold?" | "Submit your parts request online. We'll call you back with availability." |
| "Need a part after hours?" | "The counter's closed, but the form is open 24/7." |
| "Don't know the part number?" | "Describe what you need — year, make, model. We'll find the exact match." |
| "Commercial account? Skip the line" | "Set up a commercial account. Submit orders by form, get priority handling." |
| "Drove 20 minutes and it wasn't in stock" | "Check availability online before you drive in." |

### Color Application Principles

1. **Red is the hero, but use it sparingly.** Racing red draws the eye — use it for CTAs, key accents, and the logo "REQUEST" word. Too much red becomes noise.
2. **Charcoal provides the anchor.** Headings, body text, navigation, secondary UI — charcoal grounds the brand. Red pops against it.
3. **Silver bridges industrial + digital.** Metallic silver tones connect the mechanical auto parts world with clean digital surfaces.
4. **White/lighter grays = content areas.** Keep cards and page bodies in near-white so red and charcoal stand out.
5. **Green is reserved for stock status only.** Success green signals availability — don't use it for decorative purposes.
6. **Accessibility:** All primary text/background combos meet WCAG 2.1 AA (4.5:1 minimum). Racing Red `#CC0000` on white achieves 5.2:1 — pass for normal text. Red on dark backgrounds uses `#E53935` or `#FF5252` for adequate contrast.

## Typography

### Primary Font: Barlow Condensed

[Barlow Condensed](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow+Condensed) is a bold, industrial condensed sans-serif designed for high-impact display. It's the primary brand font for headings, navigation, and logo text.

| Role | Weight | Size | Letter-spacing | Case | Usage |
|------|--------|------|----------------|------|-------|
| Logo "PARTS" | 900 (Black) | 60px | 3px | Uppercase | Logo wordmark primary |
| Logo "REQUEST" | 700 (Bold) | 46px | 4px | Uppercase | Logo wordmark accent |
| Page heading H1 | 700 (Bold) | clamp(36px, 5vw, 52px) | 2px | Uppercase | Hero headings, page titles |
| Section heading H2 | 700 (Bold) | 28-32px | 2px | Uppercase | Feature sections, form headers |
| Subheading H3 | 600 (Semibold) | 20-24px | 1.5px | Uppercase | Card titles, feature names |
| Nav items | 600 (Semibold) | 16-18px | 2px | Uppercase | Navigation links |
| CTA buttons | 600 (Semibold) | 16-18px | 1.5px | Uppercase | All action buttons |
| Eyebrow labels | 600 (Semibold) | 12-14px | 2.5px | Uppercase | Section eyebrow text |
| Badges / pills | 600 (Semibold) | 11-12px | 1.5px | Uppercase | Status badges, tags |

**Fallback stack:** `'Barlow Condensed', 'Arial Narrow', 'Impact', 'Arial Black', sans-serif`

### Secondary Font: Rajdhani

[Rajdhani](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Rajdhani) is a clean, open sans-serif with a technical feel — works well for body text, taglines, and form content. Balanced proportions keep it readable at smaller sizes.

| Role | Weight | Size | Letter-spacing | Case | Usage |
|------|--------|------|----------------|------|-------|
| Body text | 400 (Regular) | 16-18px | 0px | Sentence | Paragraphs, descriptions, form labels |
| Body lead | 500 (Medium) | 18-20px | 0.25px | Sentence | Hero subtext, lead paragraphs |
| Small body | 400 (Regular) | 14-15px | 0px | Sentence | Form helper text, card descriptions |
| Tagline | 600 (Semibold) | 15px | 2.5px | Uppercase | Logo tagline, subtitle |
| Input text | 400 (Regular) | 16px | 0px | Sentence | Form input values |
| Placeholder | 400 (Regular) | 16px | 0px | Sentence | Form placeholder text |
| Caption | 400 (Regular) | 12-13px | 0.5px | Sentence | Footers, disclaimers, metadata |
| Data / prices | 500 (Medium) | 16px | 0px | As styled | Price displays, part numbers |

**Fallback stack:** `'Rajdhani', 'Segoe UI', 'Arial', sans-serif`

### Typography Principles

1. **Condensed for impact, standard for readability.** Barlow Condensed is for headlines and labels — it communicates confidence and industrial efficiency. Rajdhani handles the reading experience.
2. **All-caps headings with letter-spacing.** Uppercase + tracking gives the brand a technical, catalog-like feel. Don't letter-space body text.
3. **Red text should never be small.** Red `#CC0000` at sizes below 14px loses readability. Use charcoal `#333333` for any text under 14px.
4. **Avoid using both fonts for the same text block.** Headlines in Barlow, body in Rajdhani — keep the separation clean.
5. **Line height:** Body text at 1.5 minimum. Headings at 1.1-1.2.

### Logo Description

The Parts Request logo uses a **12-tooth gear** in metallic silver gradient (E8E8E8 → C0C0C0 → A0A0A0) with a racing red radial hub (FF1744 → D32F2F → B71C1C). The gear:

- Has 12 rectangular teeth evenly spaced around the outer ring
- Contains an inner ring with 6 spokes (3 with highlight strokes for depth)
- Features a subtle crossed-wrench motif inside the red hub at low opacity
- Renders a drop shadow for dimensionality on light backgrounds

Right of the gear, the wordmark reads:
- **"PARTS"** in charcoal (#2A2A2A), Barlow Condensed 900 weight, 60px, 3px letter-spacing
- **"REQUEST"** in racing red (#CC0000), Barlow Condensed 700 weight, 46px, 4px letter-spacing
- A **red accent underline** (#CC0000, 3px tall, 280px wide) separates the title from the tagline
- **Tagline:** "ashtabula's local auto parts counter" in Rajdhani 600, 15px, 2.5px letter-spacing, warm gray (#888888)

### Logo Usage Rules

- **Always use the full wordmark** for header/navigation contexts (gear + "PARTS REQUEST" + tagline)
- **Icon-only gear** is acceptable for favicons, loading states, avatar placeholders, mobile app icons
- **Clear space:** Minimum 1/4 of mark width around all sides — no text, other logos, or UI elements invading this zone
- **Minimum reproduction:** Wordmark must be at least 200px wide in digital. Icon-only at least 40px wide
- **On dark backgrounds:** Use the full-color logo as designed — the metallic silver and red hub stand out against charcoal/navy/black
- **On light backgrounds:** Use the full-color version — it was designed for white/near-white backdrops
- **Do NOT:** Recolor the gear, change the red to another color, rotate the gear, distort proportions, or separate the gear from the wordmark
- **Do NOT:** Use the logo text recreated in a different font — always use the SVG
- **Monochrome:** Acceptable for print/embroidery where full color is unavailable — use solid #333333 for the entire logo

## Design Language & Visual Direction

### Mood

**Gritty, industrial, blue-collar, confident.** Ashtabula is a working-class Lake Erie town. The brand reflects that — it doesn't try to look like Silicon Valley. Analog warmth and texture differentiate it from the slick, sterile national chain sites.

### Design Language

- **Industrial auto parts aesthetic** — gear motifs, red accent lines, silver/charcoal industrial palette
- **Subtle grain texture** — slight noise/grain overlay on hero images and section backgrounds adds analog feel
- **Warm undertones** — off-white backgrounds (#F5F5F5, #FAFAF8) instead of pure sterile white
- **Card-based content** — clean white cards on warm gray backgrounds, with red accent bars for section headers
- **Bold all-caps headings** with letter-spacing — technical, catalog-inspired typographic voice
- **Generous negative space** — let the content breathe, but keep it dense enough to feel substantial

### Hero Image

**Path:** `branding_research/parts-request/hero.jpg`
**Dimensions:** 1280×720 (16:9)
**Description:** Authentic independent auto parts shop counter — warm shop lighting, worn wooden counter, open parts manual, mechanic's hand, stocked shelves behind. Negative space preserved on left side for text overlay.
**Photography style:** Must feel real and local — not a sterile showroom. The authenticity gap vs. national chains is the selling point. Warm lighting, analog texture, genuine working atmosphere.

### Iconography

- Use **line icons** (2px stroke) in charcoal (#333333) or metallic silver (#C0C0C0)
- Mechanical/automotive metaphor family: gear, wrench, steering wheel, engine block, brake disc, battery, oil can, truck, tool box
- For UI functions: shopping cart (parts request), clipboard (order), truck (delivery), clock (availability), checkmark (stock), phone (contact)
- Avoid filled icons — maintain the industrial line-art feel
- Red icons only for CTAs and error states

### UI Patterns

| Pattern | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Parts Request Form** | Multi-field form: year/make/model dropdowns, part description textarea, contact info, submit. Red accent on active fields. |
| **Availability Check** | Search bar with typeahead suggestion. Results show "In Stock" (green pill) or "Available by [date]" (charcoal pill). |
| **Commercial Account Portal** | Login-gated form with saved vehicle profiles, order history, repeat ordering. |
| **Status Badges** | Success green for "In Stock", warm orange for "Limited Availability", charcoal for "Order Only". |
| **Featured Categories** | Grid of category cards with icons — Brakes, Suspension, Engine, Electrical, Filters, Fluids. |
| **Trust Bar** | Horizontal strip below hero showing logos (Auto Value), years in business, number of parts brands carried, delivery area. |
