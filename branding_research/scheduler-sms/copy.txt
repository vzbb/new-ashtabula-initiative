## Supporting Copy

### Value Proposition (Headline)
"Turn appointment reminders into a hands-free system that practically runs itself."

### Sub-Headline
"Reduce no-shows with configurable SMS and email reminders. Clients respond CONFIRM or CANCEL — you see results in real-time."

### Feature Highlights (Bullet-ready)
- Multi-channel reminders — SMS, email, or both
- Configurable timing: 1 hour to 1 week before
- Client self-service: reply to confirm or cancel
- Real-time delivery tracking
- No complex setup — configure once and forget it

### Call to Action Options
- "Set up reminders"
- "Start reducing no-shows"
- "Configure your reminders"

---

## Tone Notes
- **Voice:** Direct, reassuring, capable. Speaks to a busy business owner who doesn't have time to babysit a new tool.
- **Avoid:** "AI-powered", "machine learning", "smart algorithms"
- **Do use:** "Set it up once", "we'll handle the rest", "automated and dependable"
- **Key differentiator to emphasize:** This isn't just a notification tool — it's a two-way conversation. Clients can reply. You stay informed.
