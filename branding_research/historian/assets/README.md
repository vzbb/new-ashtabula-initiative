### 5. Harpersfield Covered Bridge
- **Filename:** `ash_harpersfield_bridge.jpg`
- **Resolution:** 3776 × 2266 px
- **Description:** The Harpersfield Covered Bridge, built in 1868 — the second-longest covered bridge in Ohio at 228 feet. Spans the Grand River in Harpersfield Township. Howe truss design.
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons — [File:Harpersfield Covered Bridge, Harpersfield Township, Ashtabula County, Ohio - 20220216.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Harpersfield_Covered_Bridge,_Harpersfield_Township,_Ashtabula_County,_Ohio_-_20220216.jpg)

## Underground Railroad

### 6. Col. William Hubbard House (Hubbard House)
- **Filename:** `ash_hubbard_house.jpg`
- **Resolution:** 4032 × 2268 px
- **Description:** The Colonel William Hubbard House in Ashtabula, Ohio — a known station on the Underground Railroad. The home was used to shelter freedom seekers escaping slavery before the Civil War. Now operated as the Hubbard House Underground Railroad Museum.
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons — [File:Col. William Hubbard House - Ashtabula.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Col._William_Hubbard_House_-_Ashtabula.jpg)
- **Credit:** Photographer: Nheyob
- **License:** [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) — Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
- **Attribution required:** Yes — credit "Nheyob / CC BY-SA 4.0"

## Historic & Industrial Heritage

- **Description:** The Brown Hoisting Machinery Company's "Brownhoist" Standard Bridge Tramway Plant, 1905. Illustrates Ashtabula County's industrial and heavy-manufacturing heritage — bridge-building and material-handling equipment.

## Scenic & River Views

### 9. River Scene in Ashtabula Harbor (1915)
- **Filename:** `ash_river_scene.jpg`
- **Resolution:** 6600 × 4066 px
- **Description:** A Curt Teich photochrom postcard from 1915 showing the river scene in Ashtabula Harbor, with boats and dockside activity. The message on the reverse mentions the 1918 Spanish Flu pandemic.
