# site-ops-pro Branding Notes

Target account: Brobst Earthworks, LTD

Public-facing brand observed on the current company site: `Brobst Maintenance`

## First-Pass Read

- The brand leans practical, established, and operations-first rather than flashy.
- Messaging emphasizes commercial and industrial landscape maintenance, snow removal, and long-term reliability.
- The company presents itself as an experienced local operator with more than 30 years in the region.
- Current site copy also says they are not taking new commercial or residential jobs right now, which is useful context for tone and qualification.
- The overall feel should be hardworking, dependable, and service-oriented, with a grounded industrial/earthworks tone.

## Source-Backed Notes

- Homepage headline: “Ashtabula's first choice for commercial and industrial landscape maintenance and snow removal for over 30 years!”
- Homepage body copy says Brobst has served Ashtabula County, Geauga County, and Lake County.
- Career page describes work like yard maintenance, commercial mowing, edging, weed-whacking, and snow and ice management.
- Career page says the company has been a repeat `STAR BEACON Readers' Choice Award` winning landscaper.
- Company address on the site: `504 W. 57th Street, Ashtabula, Ohio 44004`.

## Visual Direction

- Primary feel: gold, black, white, and earthy neutrals.
- The official logo is a gold wordmark/logo mark on a light background.
- Imagery skews to real jobsites, crew photos, landscaping, snow plows, and equipment.
- Avoid overly polished or corporate-slick visuals; this brand reads as local, practical, and field-proven.

## Suggested Brand Traits

- Dependable
- Experienced
- Local
- Industrial
- Seasonal
- Hardworking
- Straightforward

## Notes For Future Work

- If `site-ops-pro` should present as a lead-gen or operations portal, keep the tone pragmatic and operational.
- If the design needs to feel more premium, add polish through spacing, type hierarchy, and restrained gold accents rather than bright colors.
- If the next pass needs stronger confirmation of the exact legal entity `Brobst Earthworks, LTD`, verify it against a business filing or official contact source before using it in prominent copy.
