# Plating Tracker Pro — Brand Identity & Narrative

**Product:** Plating Tracker Pro  
**Tier:** Premium analytics extension of Plating Tracker  
**Parent Product Suite:** Plating Tracker → Plating Tracker Pro  
**Target Business:** ISO-certified metal finishing shops with quality managers who need data  
**Primary Buyer:** Quality Manager / Lab Manager (35–60, chemistry/metallurgy/mechanical engineering, CQT/CQE certified)  
**Secondary Buyer:** Operations Manager at shops running 40–150 employees with multiple plating lines  
**Industry:** Metal Plating / Metal Finishing — specifically ISO 9001, IATF 16949, Nadcap, AS9100 certified operations  
**Region:** Rust Belt / Great Lakes Manufacturing Corridor (Ohio, Pennsylvania, Michigan, Indiana, Illinois)

---

## Relationship to Plating Tracker (Base Brand)

Plating Tracker Pro is **not** a separate product — it is the analytics-forward tier of Plating Tracker. It inherits all base features (job tracking, spec management, customer portal, barcode scanning, QC checkpoints, C of C generation) and adds:

- **Bath chemistry trending** — automated graphing of pH, temperature, concentration over time
- **Defect heat mapping** — cluster visualization by part type, plating line, operator shift, cycle spec
- **First-pass yield dashboards** — real-time yield rates trended by line, operator, and part family
- **Audit-ready documentation** — every job's full lifecycle in one retrievable package

**Visual identity is shared.** The logo, hero imagery, color palette (navy, steel blue, copper), typography system, and supplementary assets (hex pattern, metal texture) are identical to base Plating Tracker. Find them at:

## Brand Story — The Pro Angle

The base Plating Tracker story is about *visibility*: knowing where every job is, replacing paper travelers, keeping the foreman's knowledge from retiring out the door.

The Pro story is about *prediction*.

The Quality Manager at an ISO-certified plating shop sits on a mountain of data. Lab analysis sheets for every bath — pH, temperature, concentration — filed in binders going back years. Reject reports that get tallied at month-end, when the pattern is already cold. Customer-specific spec sheets that might be on revision 7, but the binder on the floor still shows revision 3.

She knows the data could tell her something. She just doesn't have the tool to make it talk.

Plating Tracker Pro is that tool.

Not a LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System) that costs six figures and takes a year to implement. Not a generic BI tool that needs a data scientist to operate. Purpose-built analytics for the metal finishing floor — where "trending" means catching a pH drift before it becomes 200 scrapped parts, and "dashboard" means knowing first-pass yield by the hour, not by the quarter.

## Copy Guidelines — Pro-Specific Rules

1. **Lead with the data.** Every Pro feature description starts with what the data shows. "Automatically graph pH, temperature, and concentration" — not "Monitor your bath chemistry."
2. **Quantify the cost of not knowing.** "The cost of one major reject justifies the Pro upgrade." "Catch drift before it becomes 200 scrapped parts."
3. **Speak to the Quality Manager.** All copy assumes the reader has 15+ years in metal finishing. Use ASTM specs, micron measurements, and bath chemistry terms naturally.
4. **Audit-readiness is a headline, not a footnote.** The ISO/Nadcap/AS9100 audit is a primary buying trigger. Lead with it.
5. **Pro is an upgrade, not a separate product.** Never position Pro as a different product. It is "the analytics tier of Plating Tracker" — always reference the base to ground it.
6. **Green means pass.** Use the green Pro accent (`#38A169`) deliberately — for yield indicators, certified status, passing metrics, and Pro-tier badges. It signals "capability proven."
7. **No sales-gloss.** No "unlock," "leverage," "empower," "transform," "seamless," "best-in-class," "game-changing," "next-generation." These words are banned in Pro copy.
