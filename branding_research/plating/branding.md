## Brand Story

Metal plating is invisible infrastructure. Every car on the road has plated parts. Every aircraft. Every medical device. Every fastener, connector, and bracket that needs to survive corrosion, friction, and time.

The shops that do this work — family-owned, 40-100 employees, ISO-certified, 50+ years in business — run on paper travelers and whiteboards. A foreman with 40 years of tribal knowledge walks the floor and knows every job by heart. But that foreman is retiring. OEM customers are demanding digital quality documentation. And the owner is caught between growing the business and losing institutional knowledge.

Plating Tracker exists to close that gap.

Not an ERP overhaul that takes 18 months and six figures. Not a generic project management tool that doesn't understand ASTM specs. A purpose-built system that speaks chemistry and microns — that treats a barrel load of zinc-plated fasteners the same way the shop floor does: as a job with a traveler, a spec sheet, inspection checkpoints, and a certification at the end.

Built for Ashtabula County. Ready for the Rust Belt.

### Service Descriptions

**Job Tracking:**
> From receipt to shipping, every job gets a digital traveler. Scan a barcode at each process step — pretreatment, plating, inspection, QC hold, ship. Know exactly where every load is, in real time.

**Spec Management:**
> Store customer-specific plating specs with revision control. Operators always see the current approved cycle — never an outdated binder page. Supports ASTM, AMS, customer-specific, and military specs.

**Quality Documentation:**
> Automated Certificate of Compliance generation tied to QC checkpoint signoffs. Plating thickness reports, salt spray results, and inspection data — all pulled from the job's digital record, not recreated from paper.

**Customer Portal:**
> Your customers get a login. They place orders, upload part drawings, check job status, download certifications. Fewer phone calls for you. Better visibility for them.

**First-Pass Yield Dashboards (Pro):**
> Real-time first-pass yield dashboards updated with every QC scan. Spot processes trending toward reject thresholds before they produce scrap. Continuous improvement driven by live data, not monthly spreadsheet dumps.

**Certification Generation (Pro):**
> One-click C of C generation with electronic signature. Audit-ready documentation packages for ISO, Nadcap, and customer audits. Every job's full lifecycle in one retrievable record.

**Bath Chemistry Trending (Pro):**
> Automatically graph pH, temperature, and concentration data from lab analysis. Detect drift before it causes a reject. Trend lines across weeks and months — not just the last panel.

**Defect Heat Mapping (Pro):**
> See where defects cluster — by part type, plating line, operator shift, or cycle spec. Identify root causes instead of fighting symptoms.

## Industry Language & Terminology

Use these terms naturally throughout copy and UI. They signal "you know our world."

### Plating Processes

### Quality Standards

### Shop Floor Terms
First-pass yield, Reject rate, Cycle time, Barrel load / Rack load, Thickness (microinches / microns), Pretreatment / Preplate, Bath chemistry, Traveler, ASN, First Article Inspection

## Visual Guidelines

### Color Palette Reference

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#1B2A4A` | Primary brand color — headers, nav, primary elements |
| Steel Blue | `#3B6FA0` | Interactive elements, links, secondary buttons |
| Copper | `#C87533` | CTAs, highlights, accent — references plating chemistry |
| Charcoal | `#2D3748` | Body text |
| Slate | `#718096` | Secondary text, muted labels |
| Light Gray | `#E2E8F0` | Borders, dividers |
| Off White | `#F7FAFC` | Page/card backgrounds |
| Bronze | `#A0522D` | Premium accents sparingly |

### Typography Reference

| Role | Font Stack | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Headings | `Industry`, `Bebas Neue`, `Oswald`, `Impact` | 400, 700, 900 |
| Body | `Inter`, `Roboto`, system sans-serif | 300, 400, 500, 600, 700 |
| Monospace | `JetBrains Mono`, `Fira Code`, `Consolas` | 400, 700 |
| Measurement | Same as monospace — always use mono for values | 400 |

Headings are condensed and often uppercase. Body is clean and highly legible. Measurement values always appear in monospace: `1.2 mil`, `pH 4.8`, `250°F`.

### Logo Usage Rules

1. Always maintain 16px minimum clear space on all sides
2. Full-color logo on white/light backgrounds — never on busy photography
3. White logo variant on navy (#1B2A4A) or charcoal backgrounds
4. Minimum print size: 0.5 inches wide (horizontal lockup)
5. Minimum screen size: 32px height for icon mark, 120px width for lockup
6. Never stretch, rotate, apply drop shadows, or recolor logo elements
7. The copper accent may be replaced with white for one-color applications

## Target Buyer Personas (Brand Lens)

### Primary Buyer: The Owner / Operations Manager
- **Brand relationship:** Needs to trust that this software was built by people who understand plating. Skeptical of SaaS — wants to see it work on his floor. Price-sensitive but will pay for proven reliability.
- **Message angle:** "Replace the walk. Know every job. Don't lose the tribal knowledge."
- **Tone:** Direct. Respect his experience. Show, don't tell.

### Secondary Buyer: The Quality Manager
- **Brand relationship:** Technical champion. She'll evaluate the spec management, cert generation, and audit-readiness. If she signs off, the owner buys.
- **Message angle:** "Your next audit should take hours, not days. Automate cert generation. Trend bath chemistry before it drifts."
- **Tone:** Detailed. Technical. Give her the spec sheet.

### End-User: OEM Procurement / Supplier Quality
- **Brand relationship:** Customer portal user. Experiences the brand through self-service — login, place order, check status, download cert. If it's easy, they push the plating shop to adopt it.
- **Message angle:** "Stop calling for status updates. Self-serve orders, certs, and tracking."
- **Tone:** Professional. Reliable. Reduce friction.

### End-User: Small Fab Shop Owner
- **Brand relationship:** Price-sensitive. Wants quick quotes and easy re-orders. Cares about finish consistency between batches.
- **Message angle:** "Quick quote. Fast turnaround. Match the last batch."
- **Tone:** Plainspoken. No jargon.
