## Research Notes & Caveats

- **Founded 1853**: The County Commissioner page states the business was founded in 1853. The original task card mentioned "est. 1898" — the 1898 date may refer to a different milestone (incorporation, a specific location opening, etc.). 1853 is the canonical founding year from the official biography.
- **ducrofuneralservice.com does not resolve**: The correct domain is **ducro.com**. The ducrofuneralservice.com domain was never active in Wayback Machine history.
- **JS-rendered site**: All content research was done via JSON-LD structured data extraction and secondary sources (county directory, pre-planning portal, Wayback Machine). Live page content is loaded dynamically via Vue.js and not accessible via curl.
- **Facebook/LinkedIn**: Not directly verified. The site references Facebook in its Open Graph tags but no specific page was confirmed.
- **Revenue/employee count**: Not available from public sources.
