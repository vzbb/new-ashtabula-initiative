New Ashtabula Initiative builds focused prototypes for civic offices, small businesses, service companies, cultural groups, and community organizations across Ashtabula County.

75working MVP routes maintained from one canonical map

Software experiments with a practical sales target.

The project is a live workshop for turning local friction into useful software: permit guidance, quote tools, scheduling flows, visual planners, service dashboards, document assistants, and outreach-ready demos. Each MVP is intentionally narrow enough to understand, test, and improve without losing the buyer context that made it worth building.

Civic and public service

Tools for permits, zoning, eligibility, public information, and service navigation.

Trades and local services

Estimators, booking flows, repair queues, and customer intake for service businesses.

Industrial and logistics

Parts requests, quote workflows, tracking tools, and operational assistants.

Tourism and culture

Guides, planners, docent concepts, creative portfolios, and visitor-facing experiments.

Built to be inspected, not hidden behind pitch language.

  • Routes are managed from a structured sitemap so deployment, screenshots, and analysis stay aligned.
  • Each MVP remains independently editable under the monorepo instead of being trapped in one large app.
  • Visual review and AI analysis workflows help triage the full portfolio without manually opening every route.
